Atom cards that build molecules when you put them together

Each card is an atom. Point the camera at them and 3D atoms appear on the cards — slide cards next to each other and they bond into a molecule that the camera names. Markers can work together, which photos and posters can’t.

Ready for atom cards

Print the cards (or open them on another screen), press “Start camera” and show two or more cards at once.

Tracking runs on this device. No camera images are recorded or uploaded.

Where cards that interact are useful

  • Science teaching

    Students build molecules by hand and see the result, in class or at a science fair — without screens to share.

  • Trading and board games

    Cards that react when placed together: characters that team up, items that combine, maps that connect.

  • Museums and workshops

    Stations where visitors combine physical pieces and the screen explains what they made.

Photos and posters (the Print AR and Scavenger Hunt demos) look nicer; markers are steadier, cheaper to scale and can work together.
