How we work

A clear process, with something real on a device early

  1. 01

    Discover

    We agree on who the experience is for, which devices they use, and what a successful outcome looks like.

  2. 02

    Prototype

    A working link on real phones or headsets within the first sprint, so decisions are made on something you can hold.

  3. 03

    Produce

    3D assets, interaction and front-end built to performance budgets, tested across iOS, Android, headsets and desktop.

  4. 04

    Launch & hand over

    Deployment, documentation and source files. You own the code and the glTF assets.
