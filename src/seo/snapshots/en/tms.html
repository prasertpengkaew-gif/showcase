Reading the board

What each number and status means

The rules are simple and the same for every trip, so dispatchers can trust the board at a glance.

Scheduled
The trip has not left yet. It shows 0% and the time it would take if it departed now.
In transit
The trip is moving and is less than 30 minutes behind plan. A small delay is shown but not flagged.
Delayed
The trip is 30 minutes or more behind plan. It turns orange and counts in “Delayed now” so dispatch can step in.
Delivered
The trip has arrived and stays on the board with how late it was, if at all.
Arrives in
Worked out from the speed the trip is actually achieving, not just the original plan, so it moves as the trip runs.
On-time rate
The share of delivered trips that arrived within 30 minutes of plan. It is empty until the first delivery.

Who uses it

One system, four kinds of user

  • Dispatcher

    Plans trips, assigns vehicles and drivers, and watches the board for late trips.

  • Driver

    Opens a link on a phone to start a trip, report stops and take a photo as proof of delivery.

  • Customer

    Follows their own shipment from a tracking link, without an account or a call to the office.

  • Manager

    Reviews on-time rate, trips per vehicle and cost per trip, and exports data for accounting.

How data flows

From the order to the report

  1. 01

    Order in

    Orders arrive from your sales system, a spreadsheet import or a web form.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Orders are grouped into trips, and a vehicle and driver are assigned with a planned time.

  3. 03

    Track

    GPS devices or the driver’s phone report position. The board turns that into progress, delay and ETA.

  4. 04

    Deliver and report

    Proof of delivery is saved, the customer is notified, and the trip feeds the reports and your accounting system.
