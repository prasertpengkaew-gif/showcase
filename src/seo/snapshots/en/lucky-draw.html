A simple party idea

How it works

Guests scan a QR code at the entrance and type their name. When it is time, the host spins the wheel on the big screen.

Good to know

Fair and visible

The winner is chosen at random and the whole room watches the wheel, so nobody doubts the result.

No double wins

Past winners come off the wheel, so the prizes spread across more guests.

Concept only

A real event needs a server to hold the guest list and a check against fake or repeated sign-ups.
