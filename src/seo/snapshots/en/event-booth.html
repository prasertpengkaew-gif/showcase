What we add for a real event

This demo shows the booth itself. These parts need a small server or extra hardware, so they are quoted per event:

  • QR download

    Each photo gets a QR code on the booth screen; guests scan it to get the picture on their own phone. Included in the booth rental system — see the kiosk demo.

  • Instant printing

    A connected photo printer prints each picture with the event frame in seconds.

  • Live photo wall

    New photos appear on a big screen at the venue, with moderation.

  • Branded 3D props

    Product, mascot or logo props made from your artwork, tracked to the face or body.

  • Opt-in lead capture

    Guests can leave an email or LINE ID to receive their photo — with clear consent.

  • Engagement report

    Photo, share and visit counts during the event, for your sponsors and recap.
