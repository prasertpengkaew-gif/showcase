A simple New Year idea

How it works

Guests scan a QR code at the venue or on a greeting card. The dragon appears on the floor at life size, and they can walk around it, take a photo, and send the greeting.

Good to know

Works on most phones

AR runs on recent iPhone and Android phones. Other devices still see the dragon in 3D on screen.

Made to order
