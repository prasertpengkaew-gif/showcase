How it works

  1. 1

    Choose a model

    Pick the chair, robot or car. Each loads as a real glTF file.

  2. 2

    Explore in 3D

    Drag to orbit, pinch or scroll to zoom, or use the buttons below the viewer.

  3. 3

    View in your space

    On a supported phone, tap the AR button and aim at the floor to place it.

Device compatibility

Device Mode & requirements
iPhone & iPad Apple AR Quick Look Safari (or Chrome) on iOS 12+. Some in-app browsers block it.
Android WebXR or Google Scene Viewer Chrome on a device certified for Google Play Services for AR (ARCore).
Desktop & other devices Interactive 3D Any modern browser with WebGL. AR is not available on desktop.

AR requires a secure HTTPS connection and camera permission. Android's Scene Viewer downloads the model from this site's public URL, so it won't work from a local development server.

For events

Using this at your event

  • A photo with a giant mascot

    Guests place your mascot or product at real size next to them, then take a photo with the camera button in AR (iPhone) or a screenshot.

  • Launch a product at real scale

    At a launch, everyone scans a QR code and puts the new car, machine or furniture on the floor around them.

  • Branded photo booth

    For a booth with a fixed screen, an AR photo booth adds your frame, props and a smile-to-snap countdown.
