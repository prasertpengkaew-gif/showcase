About

TheNerdStudio builds 3D, AR, VR and web apps for the open web

We're a design and engineering studio for the open web: real-time 3D, augmented reality and virtual reality that people open from a link, and custom web apps that teams use every day.

Who we are

Designers and engineers who ship to real devices

We work at the point where 3D content, web engineering and product design meet. That means we can take a project from a CAD file or product photo to a fast, tested AR or VR experience without handing it between agencies.

We favour the browser over native apps because it removes the biggest barrier to AR and VR: asking people to install something. Everything we build runs from a URL on phones, headsets and desktop.

The same engineering builds web apps beyond 3D: dashboards, logistics and warehouse tools, and assistants that answer from company documents.

The projects on this site are studio concepts built with openly licensed sample assets and sample data, so you can see — and try — exactly how we work.

What we believe

Three principles behind every build

Useful before impressive
AR and VR should answer a real question — does it fit, how does it work, what does it look like — not just look clever in a demo.
Honest about limits
Not every device supports AR or VR, and not every project needs it. We say so early and design the fallback as carefully as the headline feature.
Open by default
We build on open standards so your assets and code outlive any single vendor or platform.
