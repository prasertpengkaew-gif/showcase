Marker AR · industry

Point at a machine and see its status — on the machine

A marker sticker on each machine tells the camera which one it is. The phone then shows that machine’s live status, temperature and output on top of it, and the service steps when something is wrong. Same data as the Digital Twin, but where the work happens.

Simulated data. This demo runs on a built-in simulator — the readings are realistic but not from a real factory.

Ready for machine stickers

Print the stickers (or open them on another screen), press “Start camera” and point at one.

Tracking runs on this device. No camera images are recorded or uploaded.

Where machine tags help

  • Operators and visitors

    Anyone with a phone sees what a machine is doing and what to do — no login to a control screen, no walking to a terminal.

  • Maintenance rounds

    Scan a sticker to see the machine’s state and the steps for the job, hands free once the phone is on a stand.

  • Training new staff

    Walk the line and learn each machine with its readings, safety notes and procedures shown on top of it.

In this demo the readings are simulated and each sticker is linked to a machine in a small table (src/data/tags.ts). With a real plant, the same stickers read live data from your gateway — see the Digital Twin page for how to connect it.
