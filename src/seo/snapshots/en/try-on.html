What this demo is — and isn’t

  • Hats, jewellery and watches work well with tracking like this.
  • The T-shirt is a flat overlay that follows your shoulders and hips. It does not drape or fold like real fabric.
  • Production try-on uses your real product photos or 3D models, tuned for each item.

Where virtual try-on helps

  • Eyewear, hats & accessories

    Shoppers see fit and style on themselves before buying online.

  • Jewellery & watches

    Necklaces, earrings and watches placed on the body with a single tap.

  • Merch & event booths

    Branded tees and caps in a photo booth that guests save and share.
