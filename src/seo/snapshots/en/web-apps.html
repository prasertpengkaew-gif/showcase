Live apps

Real apps you can open now

Each one is a working product, not a mock-up. Open it and try it.

  • AR Photo Booth Studio

    An event kiosk that takes the photo, adds AR effects, and gives guests a QR code to download it.

    • Guests see themselves with a branded event frame and AR props such as sunglasses or a crown.
    • Smile to snap: holding a smile starts a 3-second countdown, hands-free.
    • A QR code lets each guest download their photo on their own phone.
    • Staff set up events from an admin tool, and photos queue on the device if the network drops.
    • Kiosk
    • QR download
    • Supabase backend
    • Admin tool
    • Offline queue

    Best for: Weddings, product launches, brand activations and exhibitions.

  • Factory Digital Twin

    A live dashboard of a production line in 3D with machine status, temperature trends, uptime and an alert log.

    • A 3D production line with six machines, each with a stack light in its status colour.
    • Click a machine to see its readings and a 60-second temperature chart against its warning limit.
    • An event log and a table show what changed and when.
    • Runs on a built-in simulator, and takes a live feed over WebSocket when connected to a plant.
    • Real-time data
    • Dashboard
    • 3D scene
    • Alerts

    Best for: Factories, plant managers and machine builders who want one screen for the whole line.

  • 3D Interactive Map

    A 3D city map with place search, routes and walking areas, built to be embedded in a project site, campus or venue guide.

    • A sample city with 21 places in 7 categories, a river, bridges and about 800 buildings.
    • Search in Thai or English, fly to a place and get a route from your chosen start.
    • Shows how far you can walk in 5, 10 or 15 minutes.
    • Day and night views, a minimap and keyboard controls.
    • 3D map
    • Search
    • Routing
    • Embeddable

    Best for: Property projects, campuses, malls and venues that want a map visitors can explore.

  • Motion Game for Booths

    A camera-controlled game for big screens: players move their body to play, and a leaderboard keeps the crowd competing.

    • A 30-second catch game controlled by your body, or by mouse, touch or arrow keys.
    • Raise both hands above your head to start a round, so a booth needs no staff.
    • A top-5 board kept on the device gives the crowd a reason to try again; a board shared across screens needs a server.
    • Body tracking runs in the browser on the device.
    • Camera input
    • On-device processing
    • Leaderboard

    Best for: Booths, exhibitions and shop events that need to draw a crowd.

  • Enterprise AI Assistant (RAG)

    Ask questions in Thai or English and get answers quoted from company documents, with sources, limited to what each role may read. The demo uses sample documents.

    • Ask in Thai or English and get the best passage from company documents, with its source.
    • Choose a role (employee, HR or finance): documents that role may not read are never searched.
    • Says “not found” instead of guessing when the documents do not answer the question.
    • The demo searches five made-up documents and calls no LLM; a real system adds one to phrase the reply.
    • LLM
    • RAG
    • Access control
    • Thai and English

    Best for: HR and policy questions, internal knowledge bases and support teams.

  • Transport Management System (TMS)

    A dispatch board for a trucking fleet: live progress, delay alerts, ETAs and on-time rate, usable on a desk or on the driver’s phone. The demo uses sample shipments.

    • Eight sample trips move on a simulated clock, from scheduled to delivered.
    • A trip 30 minutes or more behind plan is flagged as delayed.
    • Arrival time is worked out from the speed each trip is actually achieving.
    • Shows trips on the road, delays, deliveries and on-time rate, with a route view for each trip.
    • Dispatch board
    • Live tracking
    • Delay alerts
    • Driver app

    Best for: Trucking and delivery operations that dispatch from phone calls and spreadsheets today.

  • Cold Room Locator

    Records where pallets sit in each cold room and recommends the picking order that moves the fewest pallets. A concept demo with sample stock, not a delivered client project.

    • A floor plan of two sample cold rooms, each with four lanes of up to three pallets.
    • A pallet is blocked by the ones in front of it, so those must be moved out first.
    • The plan picks the pallets that need the fewest moves, and the oldest first on a tie.
    • Stock only drops when you confirm the dispatch, not when pallets are lifted or moved.
    • Floor plan
    • Picking order
    • Stock control
    • Audit trail

    Best for: Cold storage and warehouses that lose time or accuracy on picking and stock counts.
