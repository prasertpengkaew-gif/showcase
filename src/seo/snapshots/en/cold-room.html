How it decides

The rules behind the recommendation

Simple rules your team can read, check and change, so nobody has to trust a black box.

  1. 01

    Blocked by what is in front

    A pallet cannot be lifted until the pallets between it and the door are moved out.

  2. 02

    Fewest moves first

    The plan picks the pallets that need the fewest pallets moved out of the way.

  3. 03

    Oldest first on a tie

    When two pallets need the same effort, the one received earlier goes first.

  4. 04

    Put back, then confirm

    Moved pallets return to their place. Stock drops only when the dispatch is confirmed.

What the full system covers

More than a picking order

The demo shows one part. A full system also covers the daily work around it.

Rooms and floor plans
Set up rooms, slots and rows to match your real cold rooms, with an interactive 2D plan.
Receiving and search
Record incoming stock with its location, and find any product across all rooms. Barcode scanners that type the code work too.
Daily pick lists
Managers set what to pick, supervisors set weights, and each step is recorded from lift to final dispatch.
Roles and sign-in
Staff, supervisor and manager roles, with an interface in the languages your team uses.
Audit history
Every receive, move, put-back and dispatch is logged with who, when, where and why.
Reports and exports
Stock by date, room and product, items waiting to be put back, and open jobs, exported to Excel, PDF or image.

The recommendation depends on a correct floor plan and the right blocking rules, which we agree with you in writing before building. It does not use cameras or AI models, and it does not connect to ERP or sales systems unless we scope that separately.
