Our designs

Two app designs we made ourselves

Both are design projects, not released apps. They show how we plan screens, flows and visual style before any code is written.

  • Fashion · AI styling app

    Rui Ya (睿雅)

    Screen designs for a fashion app that scores an outfit from a photo, suggests improvements and builds a wardrobe and weekly plan. Designed for Chinese users, with Chinese interface text and local share and payment options.

    • iOS frame
    • Flutter hand-off
    • Chinese UI
    • Onboarding
    • Paywall

    Design only: 34 screens in 21 groups on one canvas. The app itself has not been built from this design.

    What is in it

    • First-time flow: four onboarding slides, login or register with WeChat or phone.
    • Home with a daily tip, the latest result and quick shortcuts, shown for free, pro and new users.
    • Guided photo capture, an analysis loading screen, and a result with a score ring, a four-part breakdown, advice and ready-made captions for sharing.
    • Wardrobe with category and colour filters, add-item steps, search, and an outfit builder with six slots.
    • Weekly planner, virtual try-on with before and after views, a share card, history charts and empty states.
    • Paywall with monthly and yearly plans, settings, and three colour themes that change the accent colours.
    • Developer notes on the canvas: a theme-constants rule and how the result gradient is built.

    Selected screens

    • Home: daily tip, latest result and shortcuts
    • Result: score ring, breakdown and advice
    • Wardrobe: filters and item cards
    • Weekly outfit planner
    • Virtual try-on with garment swap
    • Outfit builder: six slots

  • Pets · health and care app

    Mochi Pet Passport

    An iPhone app design that keeps a pet’s health, diet and safety information together, like a passport you carry. Built as a clickable prototype so the flow can be tried, not just looked at.

    • iOS prototype
    • Tab navigation
    • Colour themes
    • Dark mode

    Design only: a clickable prototype, not a released app.

    What is in it

    • Five tabs in a floating bottom bar: Home, Health, Diet, Safety and Profile, with smooth screen changes.
    • A warm, calm look using a serif heading font with a clean sans-serif for details.
    • Several colour themes you can switch while using the prototype, including a dark one.
    • Built to be reviewed on a phone-shaped frame, so spacing and touch sizes are judged at real size.
