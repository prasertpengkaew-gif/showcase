A simple launch-event idea

How it works

A camera watches the space in front of a screen or a wall projection. Where people move, light bursts out, so the crowd becomes part of the picture.

Good to know

Draws a crowd

A wall that reacts to people pulls them in from across the hall, and they stay to play.

Private by design

It only measures where pixels change. It does not recognise faces and saves no video.

Needs hardware

A real installation needs a projector or large screen, a camera, steady lighting and a site visit.
