How an event runs

From the brief to the last guest

  1. 01

    Before

    • We agree the goal, the audience and where AR will sit in the venue.
    • We design frames, posters and cards in your brand.
    • We test the prints and screens in the venue’s lighting and signal.

  2. 02

    During

    • Guests open a link or scan a QR code, with no app to install.
    • A kiosk screen runs full screen and resets for the next guest.
    • In the photo booth studio, photos queue on the device if the connection drops.

  3. 03

    After

    • Guests keep their photos from the QR link.
    • We agree beforehand how long photos are kept and who can see them.
    • We review what worked and what to change for the next event.

Good to know

What to plan for

Guests need a phone and a signal
Most recent phones work. Older ones may show a 3D view instead of AR. Check the venue’s Wi-Fi or mobile signal early.
Prints need good light
Image tracking works best on flat, matte prints. Glare from spotlights on glossy prints can stop it, so we test your print on site.
Where photos are stored
The free demo keeps photos on the device. The photo booth studio uploads them to storage so guests can scan a QR code and download them on their own phones.
Staff and screens
Hands-free ideas need little staffing. A kiosk still needs a screen, a stand and someone to check it now and then.
