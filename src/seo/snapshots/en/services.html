Principles

How every engagement is run

Browser first
If it needs an app install, most people won’t try it. We build for the link people already tapped.
Performance budgets
We agree file-size and load-time targets up front and test on mid-range devices, not just flagships.
Graceful fallbacks
Every AR and VR experience has a complete 3D or image fallback so no visitor hits a dead end.
You own the assets
Source files and glTF/USDZ models are handed over in open formats you can reuse anywhere.
