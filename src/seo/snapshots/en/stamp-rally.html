A simple booth idea

How it works

Each booth shows a QR code. Visitors scan it with their phone, collect the stamp, and can choose to leave their contact details at the booths they liked.

Good to know

Why exhibitors like it

Visitors walk the whole hall, and each booth gets a list of people who asked to be contacted, not a pile of business cards.

Consent first

Details are shared only after a tick box, per booth. This keeps the list useful and fits data-protection rules.

Concept only

This demo simulates the scan. A real event needs printed QR codes, a server to keep results and a prize check at the desk.
