Where print AR works well

  • Packaging & labels

    Show the product assembled, in use, or with how-to steps right on the box.

  • Brochures & property sales

    A floor plan becomes a 3D model of the unit on the sales table.

  • Posters, menus & business cards

    Campaign posters, restaurant menus and cards that open a 3D or video experience.

For events

Using this at your event

  • Posters that come alive

    Scan the event poster or roll-up banner and your mascot or product pops out of it in 3D.

  • Backdrops & stage screens

    The same tracking works on a printed backdrop, a stage screen or a ticket — content can change per day or session.

  • A hunt around the venue

    Several posters, a stamp for each, a reward at the end — it keeps guests moving through the whole space.
