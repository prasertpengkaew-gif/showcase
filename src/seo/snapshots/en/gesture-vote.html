A simple event feedback idea

How it works

Hold a thumb for about a second to cast one vote. A short pause follows each vote.

Good to know

Light and camera

Needs a camera and enough light on the hand. It tracks one hand at a time.

Buttons always work

Use the buttons if the camera is unavailable or you prefer to touch the screen.

Sample votes only

Votes are not stored. A real kiosk would need a server to keep results.
