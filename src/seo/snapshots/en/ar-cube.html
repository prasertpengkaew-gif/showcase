A paper cube you hold in your hand — with your product on it

Print the net, fold it into a cube and show it to the camera. A 3D product appears on the cube and turns as you turn it. Six markers, one on each face, so it keeps tracking from any side.

Ready for the cube

Fold the printed cube (or open the marker on another screen), press “Start camera” and hold it up to the camera.

Tracking runs on this device. No camera images are recorded or uploaded.

Make the cube

  1. Download the sheet and print it at 100% on A4 (landscape), on matte paper.
  2. Cut along the outside, fold along the dashed lines and glue the grey tabs.
  3. Hold it in front of the camera in good light — and turn it!

Markers on this page

No printer? Point the camera at this single marker for the front face instead — you will see the product on it.

Where an AR cube works

  • Event giveaways

    A cube in every goodie bag: guests scan it later at home and see your product or mascot, keeping your brand in their hands.

  • Product demos

    Let customers hold and turn a machine, a model home or a new package without carrying the real thing.

  • Packaging and print

    The same six markers can be printed on a box, a calendar cube or a brochure insert.

Photos and posters (the Print AR and Scavenger Hunt demos) look nicer; markers are steadier, cheaper to scale and can work together.
