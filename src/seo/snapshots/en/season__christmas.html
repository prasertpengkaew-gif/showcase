Christmas and New Year

This landing is for 1 Dec – 15 Jan, every year. The address does not change, so a printed QR code keeps working.

The window covers Christmas and New Year’s Day. The dragon is a model we built; it swims in an S curve in our camera page. The face looks are simple drawings, not licensed characters. Use it as the photo spot at the end of a party, or as a greeting card guests scan.

What a season page is for

One link for the booth

Print a single QR code. During the season it opens this landing, with the hero, the floor AR and the face filters already chosen.

Same page, next festival

Halloween, Loy Krathong, and Christmas and New Year are entries in one file. Adding a season does not mean building a new page.

A straight path to a quote

The contact button opens the studio form with this season already named, so the enquiry is easy to route.
