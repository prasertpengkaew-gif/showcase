How we build it for your organization

The same four parts, connected to your real documents and systems.

  1. 01

    Connect your documents

    SharePoint, Google Drive, wikis, PDFs and databases are read, cleaned and split into passages.

  2. 02

    Index and keep it fresh

    Passages go into a vector and keyword index that updates when documents change.

  3. 03

    Respect permissions

    Search is limited to what the signed-in person may read, using your existing accounts and groups.

  4. 04

    Answer with sources

    An LLM writes the reply from the retrieved passages and cites them, and says so when it does not know.
