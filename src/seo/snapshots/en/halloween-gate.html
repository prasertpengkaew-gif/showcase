A life-size Halloween gate, and a photo to keep

A spooky wooden archway with pumpkins, cobwebs and bats, at real size. Place it on the floor through your phone camera, step into it, and take a photo that saves to your phone.

Concept demo . The gate is a model we built ourselves. Two ways to use it: real floor tracking with the system AR viewer, or our own camera page with a photo button.

Place it on the floor with real tracking

Press “View in your space” on your phone. The system AR finds the floor, keeps the gate at life size and has its own camera button that saves to your photos.

Drag to rotate · scroll or pinch to zoom · 220 × 60 × 300 cm

Open this page on a phone or tablet to use AR

Scan the QR code or copy the link. On desktop you can rotate and zoom the model in 3D; AR needs an iPhone or iPad with Safari, or an Android device that supports Google Play Services for AR.

🎃 Take a photo with the gate

Our own camera page puts the gate in front of you and has a photo button that saves to your phone. Tap the floor to place it, stand back, and let a friend take the picture.

The camera is used only while AR is open. Photos are made on your phone and are never uploaded.

A simple Halloween idea

How it works

Guests scan a QR code at the party or the shop. The gate stands on the floor at life size, a friend takes the photo, and it saves straight to the phone.

Good to know

Real floor tracking on iPhone and Android

The “View in your space” button uses the phone’s own AR, which finds the floor and keeps the gate in place as you walk around it.

Our camera page works everywhere

It estimates the floor from the phone’s motion sensors. It is a good fit for placing the gate and taking a photo from one spot, but it can drift if you walk around.
