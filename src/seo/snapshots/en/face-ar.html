Private by design

  • Face tracking runs locally with Google MediaPipe in WebAssembly.
  • No images or video are uploaded, stored or sent anywhere.
  • The camera stops when you press Stop camera or leave the page.

What face AR is used for

  • Virtual try-on

    Glasses, make-up, jewellery and hats that follow the face, on product pages.

  • Brand filters & campaigns

    Branded effects and photo booths for events and social sharing — from a link or QR code.

  • Interactive & hands-free UI

    Use expressions such as a smile or blink as input for kiosks, games and accessibility.

Face tracking estimates landmarks and expressions only. It does not identify who a person is.
