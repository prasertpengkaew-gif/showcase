Hand tracking & gestures

Control 3D with a wave of your hand

Show a gesture to your camera and the robot responds — no touch, no controller. Hand tracking runs on your device; nothing is uploaded.

Camera is off

Press Start camera, hold one hand up about an arm’s length away, and try a gesture from the list.

Use a photo instead

Runs locally with Google MediaPipe. No images or video leave your device.

Try these gestures

  • Open palm — wave hello
  • Thumbs up — thumbs up back
  • Victory sign — dance
  • Point up — jump
  • Thumbs down — shake head
  • Fist — punch
  • I love you sign — nod yes

Where touch-free control helps

  • Mall kiosks & digital signage

    Browse products or play with a brand character without touching a shared screen.

  • Trade-show booths

    A gesture game draws people in from across the aisle.

  • Hygiene-sensitive spaces

    Hospitals, kitchens and labs where people should not touch screens.
