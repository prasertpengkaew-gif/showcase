A hands-free game that pulls people into your booth

Players stand in front of a screen and catch falling stars with their hands — the camera tracks their body, nothing to touch. 30-second rounds and a top-score board keep a crowd around the booth.

Catch the stars

Start the camera and stand 1.5–2 m back so your upper body is in view. No camera? Play with the mouse or a finger.

Tracking runs on this device. No camera images are recorded or uploaded.

How to play

  • +10 Star
  • +25 Diamond (rare)
  • -20 Bomb with an × — avoid it

Items fall faster as the round goes on. Each round lasts 30 seconds.
