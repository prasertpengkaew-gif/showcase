VR Showroom

Walk through a virtual showroom

A small gallery of real-scale 3D products you can explore on any screen — and step into with a VR headset, straight from the browser.

Drag to look around · scroll or pinch to move closer

No VR headset detected on this device. You can explore in 3D here, or open this page in a headset browser.

  • Desktop and phone: drag to look around, scroll or pinch to move closer.
  • In VR: look around naturally; use the left thumbstick to move and the right thumbstick to turn.
  • Every object is shown at real-world size — the sofa is 2.2 m wide.
This is a local development address, so a phone can't open it. Deploy the site or use an HTTPS tunnel (see README) to test on a phone.

Open on a headset

In Meta Quest Browser, type or paste this address, then press Enter VR. On a phone, scan the code to explore in 3D.

http://localhost:5201/en/vr-showroom

Where it works

Device Experience
Meta Quest 2, 3, 3S, Pro Immersive VR (WebXR) Meta Quest Browser. Thumbstick movement and controller tracking.
Other WebXR headsets Immersive VR (WebXR) Any headset browser that supports WebXR immersive-vr sessions, such as Pico Browser.
Phones, tablets, desktop Interactive 3D Drag or touch to look around. No headset needed.

What VR is good for

  • Virtual showrooms

    Let buyers walk around products at real size before they visit a store or place an order.

  • Property and spaces

    Show unbuilt or unfurnished spaces at true scale, room by room.

  • Training and education

    Practise procedures or explore subjects safely, repeatedly, and without special equipment beyond a headset.
