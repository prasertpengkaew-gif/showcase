A simple team-building idea

How it works

Guests scan a QR code and join. When the host starts the round, everyone taps their phone, and the scoreboard on the stage screen moves live.

Good to know

Everyone joins in

It takes five seconds to explain and works on any phone, so even the shy guests play.

Easy to theme

The game can use your brand, team colours or a product, and rounds can be team against team.

Concept only

A real event needs a server so every phone shares one scoreboard, and checks against fake taps.
